PRIVATE LETTER · FOR SARAUNIYA
My Sarauniya, 💜

I wish you could see yourself for a moment through the eyes of someone who notices the little things about you.

Your intelligence. The way you think. The thoughtfulness behind the things you say. The way you can encourage me to take my studies seriously, even when my own mind is wandering somewhere else.

I notice the little things, my Queen. And I hope you never feel that you have to become someone else to deserve love. You don't have to perform for me. You don't have to be perfect to be precious.

I cannot promise that I will always know the right words or that I will never make a mistake. But I want to keep learning how to listen to you, how to understand you, how to be patient with you, and how to treat your heart with the care it deserves.

When life feels heavy, I want to be someone you can speak honestly with. When something beautiful happens, I want to celebrate with you. And on the ordinary days, when nothing extraordinary happens at all, I still want to appreciate the blessing of knowing you.

I ask Allah to keep correcting me, growing me, and teaching me how to love with gentleness, honesty, responsibility and mercy.

Because, Hayati, loving you is not just about saying beautiful things. It is also about the kind of man I choose to become.

Always with sincere affection,
Your Hamzain 🦁💜
A prayer from my heart to Allah

For you, I make
du’a.

🤲🏻

Ya Allah, You know what is in our hearts better than we ever could explain.

Please protect my Sarauniya, guide her, grant her peace when she feels overwhelmed, strength when she feels tired, and ease in the things that weigh upon her heart.

Make me someone who brings goodness into her life. Teach me to be patient when understanding takes time, gentle when words matter most, honest when the truth is difficult, and responsible with every trust You place in my hands.

If this love is good for our deen and our lives, bless it, purify our intentions, and make our path towards Nikkah easy, beautiful and pleasing to You.

Keep us close to You, and never let our affection for each other become a reason we forget You.

Ameen, Ya Rabb. 🤍
